How to retire a Critical Data Set

Retiring a CDS marks it as inactive. It is not deleted and can be restored if needed. CDEs belonging to it are not retired — they are unlinked so they can be reassigned to another CDS.

What happens when you retire a CDS

Effect on dependent records
Stewardship records assigned to this CDS retired
Critical Data Elements belonging to this CDS unlinked — CDS field cleared

Stewardship records are retired because they are directly bound to the CDS and have no meaning without it. CDEs are kept active and unlinked because they may contain valuable field-level metadata that can be reassigned to a new or different CDS.

Retiring a CDS

  1. 1
    Navigate to the Data and Stewardship page from the sidebar.
  2. 2
    Expand the Agency row to reveal the CDS rows beneath it.
  3. 3
    Click the eye-off icon on the right side of the CDS row. A confirmation panel appears listing the records that will be retired and any CDEs that will be unlinked.
  4. 4
    Review the summary carefully, then click Confirm retirement.
  5. 5
    The CDS is retired. Unlinked CDEs remain visible in the hierarchy under a No data set assigned group. Edit each one to reassign it to the correct CDS.
    See How to edit or retire a Critical Data Element.
Unlinked CDEs in the delta: When you export your delta after retiring a CDS, the unlinked CDEs will appear as updated records because their CDS field changed. This is correct — they will be updated in the master when your delta is merged. Reassign them before exporting if you want the master to hold the final CDS assignment.

Restoring a retired CDS

Restoring the CDS does not automatically re-link the CDEs that were unlinked. You must reassign them manually after restoring.

  1. 1
    Enable the Show retired toggle in the toolbar. The retired CDS appears faded with a retired label.
  2. 2
    Click the eye icon on the retired CDS row. The CDS is restored to active status.
  3. 3
    Edit any CDEs that should belong to this CDS and reassign them using the CDS dropdowns in the Edit CDE panel.
