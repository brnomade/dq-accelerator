How to export your changes and reset

Do this at the end of every working session, and before loading a new master file. Export captures your changes for the master steward. Reset clears the browser ready for the next session.

Part 1 — Export your changes

  1. 1
    Click Export in the left sidebar, under Database Actions.
  2. 2
    Find the Delta export card at the top of the page.
  3. 3
    Click Export my delta. A file named dq_delta_[your name]_[timestamp].json downloads to your computer.
  4. 4
    Send the downloaded file to your master steward.
If the Delta export card shows "No base snapshot found", you have no changes to export. You can proceed straight to reset.

Part 2 — Reset your data

  1. 5
    Click Reset data in the top-right of the header. A confirmation bar appears across the top of the screen.
  2. 6
    Click Yes, reset. Because you have already exported, no second prompt appears. All data is cleared from the browser and the application returns to the Import screen.
Reset is immediate and cannot be undone. Always export your delta before resetting. Once reset, any changes not exported are permanently lost.

Abandoning your changes without exporting

If you reset without exporting first, the application detects your unsaved delta changes and adds a second confirmation step.

  1. 1
    Click Reset data in the header. The confirmation bar shows how many unsaved delta changes you have and warns you to export first.
  2. 2
    Click Reset anyway to proceed. A second, stronger confirmation appears asking you to confirm that changes will be permanently lost.
  3. 3
    Click Yes, discard and reset. All data is cleared and the application returns to the Import screen.
