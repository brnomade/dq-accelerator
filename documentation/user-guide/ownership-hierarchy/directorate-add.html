How to add a Directorate

Your steward identity must be set before making changes. Go to Settings (top-right cog icon) and select your name under Steward Identity.
  1. 1
    Click Directorate in the left sidebar.
  2. 2
    Click + Add record in the top-right corner.
    Alternatively, click + Add next to a specific agency block to pre-fill the agency.
  3. 3
    Select the Agency from the dropdown.
    Required.
  4. 4
    Enter the Directorate Name.
    Optional.
  5. 5
    Enter the Acronym.
    Optional.
  6. 6
    Click Add. The directorate appears under its agency.
The agency must already exist before you can add a directorate to it. See: How to add an Executive Agency.
