How to remove an Executive Agency

Your steward identity must be set before making changes. Go to Settings (top-right cog icon) and select your name under Steward Identity.
  1. 1
    Click Organisation in the left sidebar.
  2. 2
    Find the agency card you want to remove.
  3. 3
    Click the eye-off icon on the right of the agency card header. The agency is retired immediately.
Removing an agency retires it — it is hidden from active lists and can no longer be selected in forms. It is not permanently deleted.

To see retired agencies, enable Show retired on the Organisation page. To restore a retired agency, click the eye icon that appears next to it.
