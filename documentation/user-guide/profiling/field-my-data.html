How to scope the view to your own data

The My Data toggle narrows the Profiling view to show only the tables and fields that belong to data sets assigned to you as a steward. Use it to focus on your own responsibilities during a profiling session.

Your steward identity must be set before using My Data. Go to Settings (top-right cog icon) and select your name under Steward Identity. If your identity is not set, the My Data button is greyed out and cannot be clicked.
  1. 1
    Click Profiling in the left sidebar, under Data Quality Elements.
  2. 2
    Click the My data pill button in the filter bar. It is located between the search box and the status filter dropdown. The button highlights when active.
  3. 3
    The view narrows to tables and fields from data sets where you are recorded as a steward. Tables from other stewards’ data sets are hidden.
  4. 4
    To return to the full view, click My data again to deactivate it.
The My Data preference is saved in the browser. If you close and reopen the application, the toggle will be in the same state as when you left. The setting is stored per browser and does not affect other stewards.
