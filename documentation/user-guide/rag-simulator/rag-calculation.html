How the RAG rating is calculated

The RAG rating for a rule allocation is determined by comparing the actual pass rate of a measurement against a threshold derived from the CDE's criticality. Higher-criticality fields require a higher pass rate to stay GREEN. A bumper value can raise the threshold further for specific rules.

Stage 1 — Criticality group scores

Each CDE can have one criticality rating per group (for example OPS, POL, REP, STRAT). Each level maps to a numeric score:

Criticality levelScore
Very Low0
Low6
Medium12
High17
Very High25

Stage 2 — Overall criticality score

The overall score is the average of the scores across all criticality groups assigned to the CDE. It falls on a scale from 0 to 25.

Formula
Overall score = average of all group scores

For example, a CDE rated OPS: High (17), POL: Medium (12), REP: Low (6), STRAT: Very Low (0) gives an overall score of (17 + 12 + 6 + 0) ÷ 4 = 8.75.

Stage 3 — Relative score

The overall score (0–25) is mapped to a relative score (0%–100%) on a linear scale. Each integer point on the 0–25 scale maps to a fixed percentage step of 4%.

Scale (selected values)
0 → 0%    5 → 20%    10 → 40%    15 → 60%    20 → 80%    25 → 100%

An overall score of 8.75 rounds to 9, giving a relative score of 36%. This becomes the baseline GREEN threshold: a rule on this CDE must achieve a pass rate of at least 36% to be GREEN.

Stage 4 — Bumper adjustment

Each rule allocation has a bumper value from 1 to 5. The bumper raises the GREEN threshold toward 100%, allowing stricter pass-rate requirements for rules that matter more.

Formula
Adjusted threshold = relative score + ((bumper − 1) ÷ 4) × (100% − relative score)
BumperEffect
1No adjustment. GREEN threshold = relative score.
2Threshold raised 25% of the way from relative score to 100%.
3Threshold raised 50% of the way from relative score to 100%.
4Threshold raised 75% of the way from relative score to 100%.
5Threshold = 100%. The rule must pass every record to be GREEN.

Using the example above (relative score 36%, bumper 3): adjusted threshold = 36% + (2 ÷ 4) × (100% − 36%) = 36% + 32% = 68%.

Stage 5 — RAG determination

The AMBER threshold is always 10 percentage points below the GREEN threshold. The pass rate (from the measurement inputs) is compared against these two thresholds:

Thresholds and ratings
GREEN threshold = adjusted threshold (from Stage 4)
AMBER threshold = GREEN threshold − 10%

Pass rate ≥ GREEN threshold → GREEN
Pass rate ≥ AMBER threshold → AMBER
Pass rate < AMBER threshold → RED

Continuing the example (GREEN threshold 68%, AMBER threshold 58%): a rule that found 700 failing records out of 1,000 would have a pass rate of 30%, which is below AMBER, giving a rating of RED. A rule with 10 failing records out of 1,000 (pass rate 99%) would be GREEN.

Full worked example
CDE criticality: OPS High (17), POL Medium (12), REP Low (6), STRAT Very Low (0)
Overall score: (17+12+6+0) ÷ 4 = 8.75 → rounds to 9
Relative score: 9 × 4% = 36%
Bumper on Rule A: 3 → Adjusted threshold: 36% + (2/4) × 64% = 68%
AMBER threshold: 68% − 10% = 58%

Measurement: 950 passing out of 1,000 → pass rate 95% → GREEN
Measurement: 620 passing out of 1,000 → pass rate 62% → AMBER
Measurement: 400 passing out of 1,000 → pass rate 40% → RED
The thresholds shown in the simulator are live — they update immediately if you change a criticality level or bumper value. No calculation is needed outside the application.
